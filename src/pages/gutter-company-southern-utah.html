Why Rain Gutters Matter in Southern Utah

Our weather swings hard. A dry month can end with a summer storm that dumps more water in an hour than the ground can take, and winter brings snow that melts in the afternoon sun and refreezes overnight along the roof edge. Without a working gutter system, that water runs straight off the roofline and soaks the ground right next to your house.

  • Summer downpours: heavy rain overwhelms undersized or clogged gutters and spills over the edge.
  • Snowmelt: meltwater needs a clear path off the roof and away from the house.
  • Freeze and thaw: water that sits in sagging gutters freezes, expands and pulls seams and hangers loose.
  • Foundations and slabs: water pooling by the foundation can lead to cracks, settling and damp basements.

Signs It's Time for New Gutters

  • Water spills over the gutter edge during a storm
  • Gutters sag or pull away from the fascia
  • Leaks or drips at the seams and corners
  • Puddles or washed out soil along the foundation
  • Peeling paint, stains or rot on the fascia and siding
  • Ice building up along the roof edge in winter
